TITLE: Options and Binomial Trees (R29)

AVA: Reading 29, valuing options. Episode five: binomial trees.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Quick basics.
ANDREW: A call is the right to buy; a put is the right to sell. Long call: loss capped at the premium, profit unlimited, breakeven X plus P. Long put: maximum profit X minus P, because a price can't fall below zero, breakeven X minus P. Short positions are the mirror image.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: How does a one-period tree work?
ANDREW: The price either goes up or down. Stock at 30, up factor 1.3333, so up is 40. Down factor is one over up, 0.75, so down is 22.5. Rate 7 percent. The up probability equals one plus r minus D, over U minus D. That's 1.07 minus 0.75, which is 0.32, divided by 0.5833: 54.86 percent.
AVA: Is that the real chance of an up move?
ANDREW: No. It's a risk-neutral probability, which is why we discount at the risk-free rate. A call struck at 30 pays 10 up and zero down. Value: 10 times 0.5486, divided by 1.07, is 5.13. The put pays 7.5 down. 7.5 times 0.4514, divided by 1.07, is 3.16.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Two periods?
ANDREW: Build the end payoffs, then work backward. Stock 50, strike 45, U 1.25, D 0.8, rate 7 percent. The up probability is 0.27 over 0.45, which is 0.6. End prices are 78.125, 50 and 32, with call payoffs 33.125, 5 and zero. Upper node: 33.125 times 0.6, plus 5 times 0.4, all over 1.07, is 20.44. Lower node: 5 times 0.6, over 1.07, is 2.80. Today: 20.44 times 0.6, plus 2.80 times 0.4, over 1.07, is 12.51.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: American options?
ANDREW: At every node, compare exercising now with holding, and keep the higher. Take a put struck at 50, stock 50, U 1.3, D 0.8, rate 3 percent, up probability 0.46. At the node where the stock is 40, holding is worth 18 times 0.54, over 1.03, which is 9.44. Exercising gives 50 minus 40, which is 10. Ten is higher, so exercise early. Today's value is 10 times 0.54, over 1.03, which is 5.24. The European put would be worth 4.95.
AVA: When does early exercise make sense?
ANDREW: For deep in-the-money puts. Never for an American call on a non-dividend stock; it's worth the same as the European call. With dividends, a call may be exercised just before the ex-dividend date.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Memory hook.
ANDREW: Up probability: one plus r minus D, over U minus D. It's risk-neutral, so discount at the risk-free rate. Payoffs at the end, then roll back. American: exercise or hold, keep the higher.
