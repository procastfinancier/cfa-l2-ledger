TITLE: Index and Bond Forwards, Futures and FRAs (R28)

AVA: Episode two. Forwards on an index and on a bond, futures, and forward rate agreements.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Why is an index different?
ANDREW: An index pays dividends almost every day, so we treat them as a continuous yield and use continuous compounding. F equals S times e to the power, r minus the dividend yield, times t. Nifty at 24,000, r of 6.5 percent, yield of 1.5 percent, 140 days. Point zero five times 140 over 365 is 0.0192. Twenty-four thousand times e to the 0.0192 is about 24,465.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: And a bond?
ANDREW: A coupon is a benefit, just like a dividend. A 7 percent bond with a full price of 1,050 pays a 35 coupon in 182 days. For a 250-day forward at 6 percent: subtract the coupon's PV, 35 divided by 1.06 to the power 182 over 365, to get 1,016. Then multiply by 1.06 to the power 250 over 365. F is 1,057.37.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Forwards versus futures?
ANDREW: Forwards are private deals. Futures trade on an exchange, with a clearinghouse guarantee, margin, and daily mark-to-market. So a futures contract's value resets to zero after each daily settlement. For bond futures, at delivery the long pays the futures price times the conversion factor, plus accrued interest.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Now FRAs. What is one?
ANDREW: A forward on an interest rate. It locks today the rate on a loan that starts later. A 1 by 4 FRA expires in one month, on a loan that ends in month four, so it's a three-month loan. The long is the notional borrower and gains when rates rise. FRAs are cash settled. Rates are MRR, a market reference rate such as MIBOR or SOFR, quoted as an add-on rate on a 360-day year.
AVA: How do we find the FRA rate?
ANDREW: Divide the long period's growth factor by the short one's. Thirty-day rate 4 percent, 120-day rate 5 percent. One plus 0.05 times 120 over 360 is 1.01667. One plus 0.04 times 30 over 360 is 1.00333. Divide: 1.01329. Subtract one and multiply by 360 over 90. The FRA rate is 5.32 percent.
AVA: Settlement?
ANDREW: Notional 10 lakh rupees. At expiry the 90-day rate is 6 percent. The interest saving is 6 minus 5.32 percent, times 90 over 360, times 10 lakh, which is 1,700. But that saving would arrive at the end of the loan, and the FRA settles at the start. So discount it: 1,700 divided by 1.015 is about 1,675, paid to the long.
ANDREW: Before expiry, find the new forward rate for the same loan period, take the gap to the contract rate, and discount from the loan's end back to today. In our notes, ten days in, that value is 1,487.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Memory hook.
ANDREW: Index: continuous. Coupon: like a dividend. Futures: reset to zero daily. Long FRA equals borrower, wins when rates rise. FRA rate: long factor over short factor. Payoff: always discounted.
