TITLE: Chapter 2 - Nifty, Bond aur Rohan ka FRA (R28)

PRIYA: Arjun के desk पर तीन clients. पहला, एक foreign fund: "Nifty पर 140 दिन का forward."
RAHUL: Index में dividends रोज़ आते हैं, तो continuous yield मानो. F equals S multiplied by e to the power, r minus yield, multiplied by t. Nifty 24,000, r 6.5 percent, yield 1.5 percent. 0.05 multiplied by 140 by 365 equals 0.0192. e to the power 0.0192 equals 1.0194. 24,000 multiplied by 1.0194 equals लगभग 24,465.
[PAUSE 1]
PRIYA: दूसरा, एक insurance company. 7 percent government bond, full price 1,050, coupon 35 day 182 पर. 250 दिन का forward, rate 6 percent.
RAHUL: Coupon equals dividend, एक benefit. F equals, 1,050 minus 35 divided by 1.06 to the power 182 by 365, multiplied by 1.06 to the power 250 by 365. यानी 1,016 multiplied by 1.0407 equals 1,057.37.
[PAUSE 1]
PRIYA: Arjun ने पूछा, forward और futures में फ़र्क?
RAHUL: Forward private deal. Futures NSE पर, clearinghouse guarantee, margin, और रोज़ mark to market. इसलिए futures की value हर settlement के बाद zero हो जाती है. Bond futures की delivery पर long pay करता है futures price multiplied by conversion factor, plus accrued interest.
[PAUSE 1]
PRIYA: तीसरा, Arjun का दोस्त Rohan. "एक महीने बाद तीन महीने का loan लेना है. RBI rates बढ़ा देगा तो?"
RAHUL: FRA करो, rate आज lock. ये 1 by 4 FRA है: 1 महीने बाद शुरू, 4 पर ख़त्म, loan 3 महीने का. Long FRA equals borrower. Rates बढ़ें तो long जीतता है. Cash settled, असली loan नहीं.
PRIYA: Rate? 30 दिन MIBOR 4 percent, 120 दिन 5 percent.
RAHUL: लंबा factor divided by छोटा factor. 1 plus 0.05 multiplied by 120 by 360 equals 1.01667. 1 plus 0.04 multiplied by 30 by 360 equals 1.00333. Divide: 1.01329. Minus 1, multiplied by 360 by 90: FRA rate equals 5.32 percent.
PRIYA: एक महीने बाद RBI ने सच में बढ़ाया, 90 दिन rate 6 percent. Notional 10 लाख.
RAHUL: Saving equals, 0.06 minus 0.0532, multiplied by 90 by 360, multiplied by 10 लाख, equals 1,700. ये loan के end पर मिलती, FRA शुरुआत में settle होता है. तो discount: 1,700 divided by, 1 plus 0.06 multiplied by 90 by 360, equals 1,700 divided by 1.015 equals 1,675.
RAHUL: Expiry से पहले value: नया FRA rate निकालो, gap multiply करो, और आज से loan के end तक discount करो. Notes में day 10 पर ये 1,487 आता है.
[PAUSE 1]
PRIYA: मंत्र.
RAHUL: Index, continuous. Coupon, dividend जैसा. Futures, रोज़ zero. Long FRA, borrower, rates ऊपर तो जीत. FRA rate, लंबा divided by छोटा. Payoff, हमेशा discount.
