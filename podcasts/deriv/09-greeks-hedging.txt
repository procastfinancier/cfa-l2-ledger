TITLE: Option Greeks, Delta Hedging and Implied Volatility (R29)

AVA: Episode nine. The Greeks.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Delta.
ANDREW: Delta is how much the option moves when the stock moves by one. A call's delta runs from 0 to 1, and a put's from minus 1 to 0. At expiry, an in-the-money call has delta 1 and an out-of-the-money call has delta 0. Call delta equals N of d1; put delta equals N of d1 minus one. If delta is 0.58 and the stock rises 0.75, the call rises by about 0.58 times 0.75, which is 0.435.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: The others.
ANDREW: Gamma is the rate of change of delta. It's highest at the money, positive for long options and negative for short ones. Delta and gamma are like a bond's duration and convexity. Vega is the sensitivity to volatility: positive for calls and puts, and largest at the money. Rho is the sensitivity to the interest rate: positive for calls, negative for puts, and minor. Theta is time decay, usually negative, with one exception: a deep in-the-money European put. The strike price has no Greek.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Delta hedging.
ANDREW: To hedge a long stock position, short calls equal to the number of shares divided by delta. For 60,000 shares and a delta of 0.6, short 1 lakh calls. If the stock rises by one, the shares gain 60,000 and the calls lose 1 lakh times 0.6, which is 60,000. The net change is zero.
AVA: The catch?
ANDREW: Delta changes as the stock moves, so the hedge must be rebalanced again and again. That's dynamic hedging, and it costs money in transactions. A sudden jump, like a big move on results day, breaks the hedge before you can rebalance. That's gamma risk. Stock has zero gamma, so to fix it, add another option, then reset delta with shares.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Implied volatility.
ANDREW: It's the volatility that makes the BSM value equal the market price. There's no closed form, so it's found by iteration. India VIX is the implied volatility of Nifty options. If you expect 25 percent and the market implies 22, options are cheap, so buy.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Memory hook.
ANDREW: Delta is the slope, gamma is the curve, vega is volatility, theta is time decay. Short calls equal shares over delta. A jump means gamma risk. Implied volatility comes from the market price.
