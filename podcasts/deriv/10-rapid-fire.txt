TITLE: Rapid-Fire Drill - Derivatives (R28 and R29)

AVA: Final episode, a rapid-fire drill. After each question, pause and answer before Andrew does.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: The forward price formula?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: S minus PV of benefits plus PV of costs, times one plus r to the t.
AVA: The forward is overpriced. What's the trade?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Borrow, buy spot, sell the forward.
AVA: Which compounding for rates, stocks and indexes?
[PAUSE 5]
ANDREW: Rates: simple, 360. Stocks and bonds: discrete, 365. Indexes: continuous.
AVA: A dividend after expiry?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: Ignore it.
AVA: Who is the long FRA, and when do they win?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: The borrower. They win when rates rise.
AVA: The swap rate formula?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: One minus the last Z, over the sum of Z.
AVA: Rates fall. Who wins in a swap?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: The fixed receiver.
AVA: Three features of a currency swap?
[PAUSE 5]
ANDREW: Two curves, a real principal exchange, and today's spot rate for valuation.
AVA: Who holds the delivery option in bond futures?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: The short. They deliver the cheapest to deliver.
AVA: The up probability?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: One plus r minus D, over U minus D. It's risk-neutral.
AVA: The rule at each node of an American option tree?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Exercise or hold. Keep the higher.
AVA: Put-call parity?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: C plus PV of X equals S plus P.
AVA: The probability on a rate tree?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: Fifty percent, with each node at its own rate.
AVA: The hedge ratio?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Change in the option over change in the stock. That's delta.
AVA: What do N of d1 and N of d2 mean?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Delta, and the probability the call ends in the money.
AVA: Dividends rise. What happens?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: Calls fall and puts rise.
AVA: When is a payer swaption exercised?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: When swap rates rise.
AVA: Long cap plus short floor?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: A payer swap.
AVA: A callable bond?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: A straight bond minus a receiver swaption.
AVA: Where is gamma highest?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: At the money.
AVA: 60,000 shares and a delta of 0.6?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Short 1 lakh calls.
AVA: A price jump breaks the hedge. What's that called?
[PAUSE 3]
ANDREW: Gamma risk.
AVA: Implied volatility is 22 percent and you expect 25. What do you do?
[PAUSE 4]
ANDREW: Options are cheap, so buy.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: The final takeaway?
ANDREW: Think no arbitrage. Same payoff, same price. Good luck on 22 November.
