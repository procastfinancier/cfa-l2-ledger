TITLE: Chapter 4 - Nifty ka Return, Infosys vs TCS aur Bond Futures (R28)

PRIYA: Foreign fund फिर आया: "Indian shares खरीदने का झंझट नहीं चाहिए, बस Nifty का return दो."
RAHUL: Equity swap. Bank Nifty return देगा, fund fixed देगा. Fixed side बिल्कुल IRS जैसी, same SFR formula. ख़ास बात: equity swap की value बीच में भी पूछी जाती है. Equity leg equals notional multiplied by, 1 plus return since last reset.
PRIYA: 1 करोड़, fixed 6.05 percent. Nifty 24,625 से 30 दिन में 24,900.
RAHUL: Return equals 275 divided by 24,625 equals 1.12 percent. Equity leg equals 1 करोड़ multiplied by 1.0112 equals 1,01,11,675. Fixed leg का PV, discount factors से, 99,40,000. Fund की value equals 1,01,11,675 minus 99,40,000 equals 1,71,675.

PRIYA: एक HNI: "मेरे पास Infosys है, लेकिन लगता है TCS बेहतर करेगा."
RAHUL: Pay Infosys return, receive TCS return. कोई pricing नहीं. Value equals, received minus paid, multiplied by notional. Infosys plus 1.3 percent, TCS minus 0.8 percent: minus 0.013 minus 0.008 equals minus 0.021. Multiplied by 10 लाख equals minus 21,000. दोनों तरफ़ से loss.
[PAUSE 1]
PRIYA: अब bond futures. पहले clean और dirty.
RAHUL: Accrued interest equals, days since coupon divided by days between coupons, multiplied by coupon. Clean price quote होता है. Dirty equals clean plus accrued, यही buyer pay करता है.
PRIYA: Meera ma'am की कहानी: sabziwala को होटल को grade A टमाटर देने हैं. कौन से देगा?
RAHUL: सबसे सस्ते. Bond futures में short कई bonds में से कोई भी deliver कर सकता है. ये delivery option short के लिए valuable है, वो cheapest to deliver देगा. हर bond का conversion factor, long pays futures price multiplied by CF.
RAHUL: Quoted futures price equals 1 divided by CF, multiplied by, full price multiplied by, 1 plus R f, to the power T, minus FV of coupons, minus accrued interest at expiry.
PRIYA: Example: bond 1,040, CF 1.13, rate 5 percent, 1.2 साल, coupons का FV 71.56, expiry पर accrued 14.
RAHUL: 1,040 multiplied by 1.05 to the power 1.2 equals 1,102.71. Minus 71.56, minus 14, equals 1,017.15. Divided by 1.13 equals 900.13.
RAHUL: Arbitrage: fair quoted price 133.65, market 135. Futures महँगा, sell futures, buy bond. Profit equals, 135 minus 133.65, multiplied by CF 0.8, equals 1.08.

PRIYA: मंत्र, और पूरी Reading twenty-eight एक line में.
RAHUL: Equity leg equals notional times one plus return. Stock-for-stock, received minus paid. Short चुनता है cheapest to deliver. Quoted futures divide by CF.
RAHUL: और पूरी reading: no arbitrage. Forward equals spot plus carry. FRA, एक loan rate का forward. Swap, FRAs का package. सब यहीं से.
