TITLE: Put-Call Parity, the Rate Tree and the Hedge Ratio (R29)

AVA: Episode six. Put-call parity, interest rate options on a tree, and the hedge ratio.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Put-call parity.
ANDREW: A fiduciary call is a call plus a zero-coupon bond paying X. A protective put is the stock plus a put. At expiry both are worth S if the stock is above X, and X if it's below. Same payoff, so the same cost today. C plus PV of X equals S plus P.
AVA: Example.
ANDREW: Stock 62, call struck at 60 costs 8, rate 4 percent. P equals C minus S plus PV of X. Sixty over 1.04 is 57.69. Eight minus 62 plus 57.69 equals 3.69. Rearranging gives synthetics: a synthetic call is stock plus put minus bond, and a synthetic put is call minus stock plus bond.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Interest rate options?
ANDREW: An interest rate call pays when the market rate is above the strike, so it gains when rates rise and protects a borrower. A put gains when rates fall and protects a lender. On a binomial rate tree, each branch has 50 percent probability, and each node is discounted at its own rate. With a strike of 5.5 percent on 10 lakh, the end payoffs are 5.2382, 1.6981 and zero percent. Rolled back node by node, the call is worth 1.9882 percent, or 19,882 rupees. In practice the payoff comes in arrears, so you'd discount one more period.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: The hedge ratio.
ANDREW: It's the number of shares per option that makes a riskless portfolio. h equals the change in the option over the change in the stock. For the 30-rupee call, 10 minus zero over 40 minus 22.5 is 0.5714. That is delta. Long h shares plus a short call gives the same value in both states, so it equals a risk-free bond, and its cost today is that payoff discounted at the risk-free rate.
AVA: And arbitrage?
ANDREW: If the call's fair value is 5.13 and the market is 6.50, sell the overpriced call and buy h shares per call, borrowing the rest. On 100 calls, the mispricing is 100 times 1.37, which is 137 today, or 147 at the year end. If the call were cheap, buy it and short h shares.
[PAUSE 1]
AVA: Memory hook.
ANDREW: C plus PV of X equals S plus P. Rate trees: fifty-fifty, each node at its own rate. Hedge ratio equals change in option over change in stock. Overpriced option: sell it, buy h shares.
