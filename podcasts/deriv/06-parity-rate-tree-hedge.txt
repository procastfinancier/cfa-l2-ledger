TITLE: Chapter 6 - Do Dost, Rate Tree aur Pehla Arbitrage (R29)

PRIYA: Arjun के दो दोस्त. Kabir ने call plus एक bond खरीदा, face value X. Sameer ने share plus put. Same strike, same expiry. Expiry पर दोनों का result same.
RAHUL: Share X से ऊपर, दोनों के पास S. नीचे, दोनों के पास X. Payoff same, तो आज cost same. Put-call parity: C plus PV of X equals S plus P. Fiduciary call equals protective put.
PRIYA: Share 62, call strike 60 at 8, rate 4 percent. Put?
RAHUL: P equals C minus S plus PV of X. 60 divided by 1.04 equals 57.69. 8 minus 62 plus 57.69 equals 3.69.
RAHUL: Rearrange करो: synthetic call equals share plus put minus bond. Synthetic put equals call minus share plus bond.

PRIYA: फिर Arjun का सबसे exciting दिन. Ma'am: "30 वाले call से एक riskless portfolio बनाओ."
RAHUL: Hedge ratio h equals, call up minus call down, divided by, S up minus S down. 10 divided by 17.5 equals 0.5714. ये delta है. Call बेचो, 0.5714 shares खरीदो. Up में 22.86 minus 10 equals 12.86. Down में 12.86. दोनों same, riskless. Long h shares plus short call equals risk-free bond.
